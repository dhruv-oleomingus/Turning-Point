Turning Point

The columns of Turning Point are cut with patterns grown by a reaction–diffusion process — two simulated substances, one feeding and one consuming the other, settling into stripes, labyrinths and spots.

Tap to start a pattern. Drag from it to stretch it — it grows where you pull it; tap elsewhere for another, and drag between them to join them.

Press play: a line crosses the plate from left to right, and every tendril it meets sounds a note in its row — the more of the pattern still ahead, the slower it goes.
