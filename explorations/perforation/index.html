Turning Point

The columns of Turning Point are cut with patterns grown by a reaction–diffusion process — two simulated substances, one feeding and one consuming the other, settling into stripes, labyrinths and spots.

Paint: tap to start a pattern; drag from it to stretch it — it grows where you pull it. Tap elsewhere for another, and drag between them to join them.

Sound: tap the cut shapes — tendrils, cells — to fill them with a colour; each colour is a voice. Tap a filled shape again to empty it.

Press play: a line crosses the plate from left to right, and each coloured shape sounds as it meets it, higher the higher it sits. The fewer the shapes still ahead, the faster it goes. You can go on painting and colouring while it plays.
