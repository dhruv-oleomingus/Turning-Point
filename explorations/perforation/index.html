Turning Point

The columns of Turning Point are cut with patterns grown by a reaction–diffusion process — two simulated substances, one feeding and one consuming the other, settling into tendrils, labyrinths and spots.

Tap to start a pattern; drag from it to stretch it — it grows where you pull it. Tap elsewhere for another, and drag between them to join them.

Press play: a line crosses the plate from left to right. Every cut shape it passes through — a tendril, a cell — sounds once, in its own voice: the small ones pluck, the middling ones play keys, the large ones bass, higher the higher they sit. The more shapes still ahead, the slower it goes. The pattern can be grown while it plays.
