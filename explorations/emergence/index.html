Emergence

In progress — the piece will play here

This sketch examines the idea of emergence through simple acts of rupture where a structure starts from a nucleus and disperses into unforeseen trajectories.

A turning point is a place of tension and negotiation. A place from where something new emerges, accumulating in latent force until that critical moment of the new. And what emerges here exceeds the sum of its parts, making its origins increasingly difficult to trace.

The name Delhi itself, which we imbue with so much significance, comes from such unsettled origins, ever evolving in the imaginations of all who have laid claim to the city. And true to its unsettled name, Delhi has remained a city in perpetual negotiation with itself, like a wobbling pillar of time, bearing successive kingdoms and cultures that have converged and flourished here.

It is this unreadability of origins, made visible only in retrospect, that allows us to make room for plurality. Here, we hold the past, present and future together without needing to resolve history into a singular sequence of causes and consequences. To recognise that what emerges is never entirely contained within what preceded it.
